# Working Conditions

A separate note from my resume (`resume_en.md`), intended to be shared with recruiters and hiring managers when discussing fit.

## Work Style

- I prefer fully remote / flexible-time arrangements. I am noticeably more productive working remotely than from an office.
- Meetings, deadlines, and delivery commitments are no issue.

## Discretionary Work

- I work better under outcome-based, discretionary arrangements than under strict fixed-hour attendance.
- Deadlines and milestones are agreed upon in advance and respected.

## Approach to Work

- I prefer organizations that value understanding the underlying mechanisms and, where possible, the users — not only "shipping fast at all costs."

## Health and Reasonable Accommodations

- I have a diagnosis of pervasive developmental disorder and hold a Japanese Mental Disability Certificate (Grade 3).
- The work-style preferences above (remote, flexible, discretionary) serve as the reasonable accommodations I would like to discuss.
- Specific terms (employment type — contract vs full-time, working pace, compensation) I would prefer to discuss individually during interviews.
